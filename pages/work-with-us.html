Join Our Team

At Golobe, we believe that great travel experiences start with great people. We're a dynamic, passionate team dedicated to making travel accessible, enjoyable, and unforgettable for everyone. If you share our love for travel and innovation, we want to hear from you.

We're looking for talented individuals across multiple disciplines including technology, marketing, customer service, and travel operations. Join us in our mission to connect travelers with the world's most extraordinary experiences.

Open Positions

We're always looking for talented people to join our team. Current openings include:

  • Full Stack Developer: Build and maintain our travel platform with modern technologies.
  • Marketing Specialist: Drive brand awareness and engage our travel community.
  • Customer Experience Expert: Deliver exceptional service to our travelers worldwide.
  • Travel Operations Manager: Curate and manage our travel offerings and partnerships.
  • Product Designer: Create intuitive and beautiful user experiences.

Send your CV to careers@golobe.com with the subject line "Application for [Position Name]".

How to Apply

  • Step 1: Review our open positions and find the role that fits your skills.
  • Step 2: Prepare your CV and a brief cover letter explaining why you'd be a great fit.
  • Step 3: Send your application to careers@golobe.com.
  • Step 4: Our team will review your application and get back to you within 5-7 business days.
  • Step 5: If selected, we'll schedule an interview and discuss how you can contribute to our mission.
